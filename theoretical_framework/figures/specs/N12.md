# Figure spec — N12 (`figures/N12_warmth.pdf`, `figures/N12_warmth.png`)

**Message.** Warmth $\kappa$ decays like $1/(W+s)$, and warm-up and real steps count alike. The W1 bound holds and is nearly tight. Warm-up removes the short-horizon (cold-window) gap but does not drive the long-horizon residual to zero; that residual is shared by SGD where it is largest.

**Layout.** 1 row × 3 panels, 13 × 4 in. PDF is vector; PNG is 300 dpi. English labels.

**Panel A — "Gap vs lr=0 warm-up W".**
- Data: `theory/v015_checks/results_v015_independent.json → groups[id=E8].data.gaps`.
- Two tasks at η=0.1: moment N=3 and parity N=2. One line per R ∈ {1, 4, 40}, with color by R and line style by task.
- x = W ∈ {0,1,2,5,10,20,50} on a symlog axis; y = `gaps.actual_Adam`.
- Dotted horizontal line per (task, R) at `gaps.tangent_affine_SGD` of W=0, labeled "SGD comparator".
- Annotate the moment N=3 values: R=1 "0.0769 → 0.0004", R=4 "0.1196 → 0.0016".

**Panel B — "Warmth decays like 1/(W+s)".**
- Data: `groups[id=E8].data.W3.per_step`, filtered to task=moment, N=3, source=P, eta=0.01.
- x = `prior_gradient_evaluations` (= W+s), log scale; y = `quantiles[2]` (median κ), log scale; drop "inf".
- One series per W ∈ {0, 5, 20, 50}.
- Dashed guide c/x, with c fitted by least squares on the W=0 series. Legend note: "median slope −1.06, R² 0.999 (η=0.01, W=0)" from `data.W3.fits`.

**Panel C — "W1: residual vs bound".**
- Data: `groups[id=E8].data.W1.panels[*]`, fields `pointwise_residual` and `r` (all messages, 24 panels).
- Log-log scatter, x = r, y = pointwise residual, colored by `eta`, marker by `task`.
- Line y = x. Text: "2,496 law pairs, max gap/bound 0.988" from `data.W1.n_law_pairs` and `data.W1.best_ratio`.

**Caption.** Left: a lr = 0 moment warm-up removes the gap between equal-moment sources at short horizons ($R=1,4$). At $R=40$ warm-up no longer drives the gap to zero; where the residual is largest, the SGD comparator is nonzero too. Middle: median warmth collapses onto one $1/(W+s)$ curve regardless of how many prior evaluations were warm-up. Right: the W1 residual never exceeds its bound $r$ and comes within 0.2% of it.
