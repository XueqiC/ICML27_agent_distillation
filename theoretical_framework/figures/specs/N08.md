**Message:** the discrepancy decays only with *independent* blocks, at exactly the exponent set by the first unmatched cumulant. Replay and descendants do not decay. The Thm. 14 bound never grows with R.

  - $`\lvert\text{iid\_gap}\rvert`$ against $`n`$, as a solid line with markers;
  - $`\lvert\text{replay\_gap}\rvert`$ against $`n`$, dashed;
  - $`\lvert\text{descendant\_gap}\rvert`$ against $`n`$, dotted.

- Axes: $`x`$ "training exposure n (blocks)", $`y`$ "|learning-value gap|". Title: "(a) Only independent blocks decay: gap ∝ n^{1−N}".
